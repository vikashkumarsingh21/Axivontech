Privacy Policy

Comprehensive information on how Axivon Technologies collects, processes, protects, and retains personal data in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act 2000.

Last Reviewed: 10/15/2026Reviewed by: Legal & Compliance Directorate
Cookie Settings

1. Introduction and Scope

Axivon Technologies ("Axivon", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website **https://axivontech.in** and the **AXIVON ONE** enterprise operational platform. We are committed to processing your personal data transparently, securely, and in strict compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**, the **Information Technology Act, 2000**, and the rules promulgated thereunder.

This Privacy Policy governs your use of our public website, client inquiry forms, customer support AI chatbots, recruitment portals, and authenticated multi-portal workspaces (including Admin, Employee, Client, Broker, and Business Partner interfaces).

2. Grounds and Principles of Processing

We adhere to the fundamental data protection principles: - **Purpose Limitation:** Data is collected solely for explicit, lawful, and specific business purposes. - **Data Minimization:** We collect only personal data strictly necessary for fulfilling stated purposes. - **Accuracy and Quality:** We take reasonable steps to maintain accurate and up-to-date information. - **Storage Limitation:** Personal data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill operational, contractual, or statutory obligations. - **Integrity and Confidentiality:** Industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards protect all data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

3. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

Depending on your interactions with Axivon, we collect: 1. **Prospective Clients & Inquiries:** Full name, business email address, phone number, company name, service interest, project specifications, and budget ranges submitted via contact and consultation forms. 2. **AI Customer Support & Chatbot:** Conversation transcripts, inquiry summaries, and contact details voluntarily provided during interactive assistance sessions. 3. **Careers and Recruitment:** Candidate name, contact details, curriculum vitae (CV/resume), employment history, education, portfolio links, and communication records. 4. **Business Partners & Brokers:** Official business name, legal entity structure, GSTIN/tax identification (where provided), contact person details, bank disbursement details (for approved commission payouts), and transaction records. 5. **Authenticated Portal Users:** Login credentials (passwords stored using salted argon2/bcrypt hashes), role-based session tokens, activity logs, task assignments, and internal communications. 6. **Technical & Telemetry Data:** IP addresses, browser user agent, device characteristics, referral URLs, access timestamps, and necessary session cookies.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We process your personal information for: - Delivering bespoke software development, AI solutions, web and mobile application engineering services. - Responding to inquiries, preparing custom Statements of Work (SOW), and issuing project estimates. - Administering user authentication, role-based access control (RBAC), and session security. - Managing recruitment, candidate evaluation, and interview scheduling. - Operating the Business Partner and Broker referral programs, including commission calculations and contract fulfillment. - Preventing security incidents, automated spam attacks, and fraudulent activity. - Fulfilling statutory compliance, tax reporting, and audit requirements.

5. Cookies, Local Storage & Tracking Technologies

Axivon uses first-party and select third-party cookies: - **Strictly Necessary Cookies:** Essential for session state, CSRF protection, and Cloudflare Turnstile anti-bot verification. These cannot be disabled. - **Preferences Cookies:** Remember user choices, such as active UI mode and dismissed announcements. - **Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics 4):** Measures aggregated visitor interactions and navigation paths. **Analytics scripts are executed ONLY after explicit affirmative consent via our Cookie Preferences modal.** - **Marketing Cookies:** We do not sell your personal data or employ invasive cross-site advertising trackers.

You may update or withdraw your cookie preferences at any time via the [Cookie Preferences](/cookie-preferences) portal or the persistent "Cookie Settings" trigger in our footer.

6. Disclosures and Sub-Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We disclose information only to vetted third-party service providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements: - **Cloud Infrastructure & Hosting:** Vercel Inc. and AWS (secure cloud compute and edge delivery). - **Database & Storage:** Managed PostgreSQL database instances with encryption at rest and in transit. - **Security & Bot Protection:** Cloudflare Turnstile for automated threat mitigation. - **Analytics:** Google LLC (only upon affirmative opt-in consent). - **Communication Infrastructure:** Transactional email and notification delivery providers.

7. Cross-Border Data Transfers

When data is transferred outside India to global cloud service providers, transfers are conducted in full compliance with the provisions of the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable cross-border transfer guidelines, ensuring equivalent organizational and technical safeguards.

8. Data Security and Safeguards

We implement defense-in-depth security measures: - Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) for all web traffic. - AES-256 encryption at rest for sensitive database stores and document attachments. - Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with principle of least privilege. - Multi-factor authentication support and automated rate limiting on API endpoints. - Vulnerability monitoring and regular automated dependency audits.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as required to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by statutory record-keeping rules: - **General Inquiries:** Retained for 24 months post-resolution unless an ongoing commercial relationship is established. - **Commercial & Project Records:** Retained for 7 years to satisfy statutory accounting, tax, and legal liability obligations. - **Job Applicant Resumes:** Retained for up to 12 months for active consideration, unless earlier erasure is requested. - **Session & Telemetry Logs:** Retained for up to 90 days for security analysis.

10. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

As a Data Principal under India's DPDP Act 2023, you hold the following statutory rights: - **Right to Access Information:** Request a summary of personal data processed by us and the processing identities. - **Right to Correction and Erasure:** Request correction of inaccurate or misleading data, updating of incomplete data, or deletion of data no longer necessary. - **Right of Grievance Redressal:** Submit grievances regarding data processing directly to our designated Grievance Officer. - **Right to Nominate:** Nominate an individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, submit a verified request through our [Privacy Requests Portal](/privacy-requests) or contact our Grievance Officer.

11. Grievance Officer & Contact Details

In compliance with the DPDP Act 2023 and the Information Technology Act 2000, our designated Grievance Officer is:

  • **Designation:** Grievance Officer & Data Protection Lead
  • **Entity:** Axivon Technologies
  • **Email:** support@axivontech.in (cc: founder@axivontech.in)
  • **Response Timeline:** We acknowledge all statutory grievances within 48 hours and provide a substantive resolution within 30 calendar days.
